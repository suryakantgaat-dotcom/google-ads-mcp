### Inferences
- The only concrete reusable patterns visible: (a) carousel/document post leading with a dollar-result headline then a staged funnel breakdown (ColdIQ); (b) polls and announcement posts that invite practitioner opinion (Hopkins); (c) a named, repeatable framework (Vallaeys "automation layering"); (d) disclosing real ROAS as a trust device (Lang, unverified). These are inferred from non-LinkedIn sources and should be checked against actual feeds.
- Authority angle: several of these people derive authority from an institutional role (Microsoft, Google, Optmyzr, Motion), which a solo practitioner lacks; named frameworks and disclosed results are the transferable substitutes.
