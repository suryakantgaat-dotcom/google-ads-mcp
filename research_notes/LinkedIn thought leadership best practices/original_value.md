# Injecting original value into LinkedIn thought-leadership posts (performance marketers)

Method note: WebFetch was blocked for every host tried (edelman.com, ppc.land, searchengineland.com, contentgrip.com, kingsley.idehen.net all failed DNS). All findings below come from WebSearch result summaries, not from reading the primary pages. Edelman figures are therefore secondary-reported and should be checked against the primary report before publication. Today's date: 2026-11-20.

## 1. What does the Edelman-LinkedIn B2B Thought Leadership Impact Report (and similar studies) say buyers value and distrust?

### Takeaway
The 2025 (7th) edition focuses on "hidden buyers" and the 2026 (8th) edition on AI. The consistent message is that buyers reward content showing real understanding of their problems, and that AI raises the premium on original data, proprietary evidence and human judgment. Weak or absent thought leadership has measurable cost: 59% of final decision-makers say it contributed to a vendor being dropped.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 report is the seventh annual Edelman-LinkedIn study, focused on "hidden buyers"; surveyed 1,934 global executives, 17 March to 3 April 2025 — [Edelman 2025 report page (via search summary)](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report); also [Demand Gen Report](https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/marketers-need-to-gear-content-to-hidden-b2b-buyers-edelman-linkedin/49901/)
- Internal misalignment in buying groups stalls more than 40% of B2B deals — [Edelman 2025](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report)
- 79% are more likely to champion a vendor in an RFP if it consistently publishes quality thought leadership — [Edelman 2025](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report)
- Hidden buyers consume about as much thought leadership as target buyers: 55% of hidden buyers vs 56% of target buyers use it to evaluate vendors; 71% of hidden buyers report little to no sales interaction — [Edelman 2025](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report)
- 60% associate unique format and design with higher quality — [Edelman 2025](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report)
- 56% of decision-makers say demonstrating clear understanding of their challenges, priorities and constraints does most to increase trust vs 14% for product strength — [Edelman 2025, as summarised in search results](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/business-marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report)
- 59% of final decision-makers say poor or absent thought leadership contributed to a vendor being removed from consideration — [Edelman 2026 page](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2026-b2b-thought-leadership-report)
- 2026 edition: eighth year, 1,135 professionals in seven markets (Edelman says "more than 1,100"); 84% used AI to summarize thought leadership during vendor evaluation; 82% say thought leadership is becoming more valuable as AI spreads — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/edelman-ai-thought-leadership-buyers/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/82-of-b2b-buyers-value-thought-leadership-more-as-ai-spreads-edelman-finds/)
- 2026 edition: buyers value original research, data and perspectives not available elsewhere, plus context, nuance and human judgment AI answers may lack — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/edelman-ai-thought-leadership-buyers/)
- Reported 72-79% of buyers say thought leadership is much more credible when customers, independent creators or independent experts help develop or amplify it — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/82-of-b2b-buyers-value-thought-leadership-more-as-ai-spreads-edelman-finds/) (range as reported by one outlet; verify)
- Only 46% of suppliers optimize for AI answers — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/82-of-b2b-buyers-value-thought-leadership-more-as-ai-spreads-edelman-finds/)
- Critiques: report does not establish that more thought leadership caused more contracts ([ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/edelman-ai-thought-leadership-buyers/)); Gaskell argues research is only original if you do not know the answer when you begin ([Substack](https://adigaskell.substack.com/p/edelman-report-downplays-the-strategic))
- Conflicting figures: 71% (one blog) vs 75% (Social Media Today citing LinkedIn) say thought leadership is more trustworthy than product sheets; 95% "more open to outreach" appears only in secondary sources — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/thought-leadership-remains-highly-valuable-for-b2b-buyers/874464/); unverified against primary report
- Distrust: no primary-source list of what buyers "distrust" was retrieved. Closest evidence is the 59% vendor-removal stat above.

### Inferences
- For a performance marketer, "demonstrating understanding of the buyer's problem" (56%) argues for posts framed around a specific account situation (e.g., lead quality vs volume), not generic tips.
- The AI findings imply first-party data from real accounts is the scarce asset; summarizable generic advice is commoditized.
- Third-party corroboration (client quotes, independent peers co-signing a test) likely lifts credibility.

### Gaps
- Could not open the primary PDFs; exact question wording and per-stat bases unverified.
- No hiring-manager-specific research found (Edelman covers buyers, not recruiters).

## 2. What content patterns do well-known performance-marketing voices use?

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings

## 3. Best practices and risks for sharing client results

### Takeaway
Legal/contract risk is the main constraint, not platform policy. A name or logo permission is not permission to publish metrics; get written consent. If unavailable, anonymize, or use own/test accounts. Claims about typical results carry FTC risk.

### Cited Findings
- NDAs often bar disclosure of any engagement information, even its existence; portfolio use must be permitted in writing — [Rocket Lawyer](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/q-and-a/answer/are-portfolio-usage-rights-different-for-confidential-projects); [Terms.law](https://terms.law/NDA/scenarios/professional-services/agency-services-nda.html)
- Suggested contract structure: logo on portfolio page allowed; written approval needed for full case studies, metrics, paid ads; options include release addendum, delayed-release clause, anonymized stories — [Mario Peshev](https://mariopeshev.com/answers/case-studies-digital-agencies/); [1827 Marketing](https://1827marketing.com/?p=2950)
- Agencies have been forced to take down unapproved case studies — [Campaign](https://campaignlive.com/article/name-shame-why-industry-names-favorite-brands-cant-talk/1968948)
- Anonymized-case-study convention: brand withheld due to NDA — [Contra example](https://contra.com/p/M6sWrL3Y-case-study-meta-ads-campaign-for-a-confidential-brand)
- Meta: a third-party tracker quotes Meta terms restricting sharing campaign data with ad networks/data brokers (even aggregated/anonymous), and making agencies responsible for advertiser compliance — [ConductAtlas](https://conductatlas.com/platform/meta-ads/meta-advertising-policies/provision/CA-P-053261/meta-advertising-data-shared-only-with-service-providers/); not an official Meta page and aimed at data brokers, not marketing posts. No Meta or Google policy specifically on publishing case studies was found.
- FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule effective 21 Oct 2024; Endorsement Guides require disclosure of generally expected results when a shown result is atypical, and "results not typical" disclaimers do not suffice — [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2024/08/ftc-issues-final-rule-on-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials); [Proskauer](https://proskauer.com/blog/on-notice-unsubstantiated-or-unrepresentative-testimonials)
- A DEV Community claim of a Meta "ad spend disclosure" rule due 2027 could not be confirmed — [DEV](https://dev.to/mattkundo/metas-new-ad-spend-disclosure-rule-what-agencies-must-do-before-2027-43ke) [Unverified]

### Inferences
- Practical guardrails: use indexed/relative metrics (percent change, ratios) rather than absolute revenue or spend; generalize industry and size; delay publication; get client sign-off in writing; state baseline and what else changed; avoid implying the result is typical.
- Check employer agreements if posting while employed at an agency or in-house.

### Gaps
- No official Google Ads or Meta policy text on case-study publication retrieved; no lawyer-authored guidance specific to performance marketers.

## 4. Forming a defensible point of view and niche positioning

### Takeaway
Sources agree: pick one narrow, non-obvious position grounded in evidence; contrarianism without substance hurts credibility. Sources are mostly practitioner blogs, not research.

### Cited Findings
- Thought leadership = defensible, non-obvious view on a real audience problem backed by knowledge others lack — [Feather](https://feather.so/blog/thought-leadership-content-strategy) (vendor blog)
- Contrarian only works if it "rings true"; not enough to be contrarian — [Animalz](https://www.animalz.co/blog/thought-leadership-content/)
- Advice to pick one contrarian view, repeat across channels, back with original research or first-hand case studies — [The Speaker Lab](https://thespeakerlab.com/blog/position-yourself-as-a-thought-leader/)
- Saying "I am a marketing thought leader" does not differentiate; specify how you stand out — [Bernoff](https://bernoff.com/blog/positioning-for-thought-leaders)
- WordStream owned an underserved niche (AdWords Quality Score) — [WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/09/21/thought-leadership-marketing) (2016, dated)
- A POV not tied to business objectives is noise — [Girard Media](https://girardmedia.com/blog/thought-leadership-content-strategy)
- A widely repeated "73% of executives trust thought leadership more than traditional marketing" stat has no traceable source — do not use [Unverified].

### Inferences
- Niche axes for a performance marketer: vertical (e.g., B2B SaaS lead gen), platform plus problem (Google Ads lead quality, offline conversion import), or method (measurement/incrementality).
- A defensible POV is one you can test publicly and update when data changes.

### Gaps
- No evidence found on how hiring managers weigh LinkedIn content versus portfolios.
- No empirical study on niche-vs-generalist personal brand outcomes in paid media.
