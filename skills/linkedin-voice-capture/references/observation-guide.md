# Observation guide

Use this to read the user's existing writing. Every item must cite the exact words it rests on. Tag each as **[Observed]** or **[Inference]**. If a feature appears once, say "once"; do not generalise from one instance.

## Confidence rule

- Under 5 samples or under 500 words in total: low. Say so at the top.
- A trait counts as a pattern when it appears in at least 3 separate samples. Otherwise report it as a single instance.
- Samples written to an AI (chat messages) and samples written for readers (posts, emails to clients) are different registers. Keep them in separate columns and do not merge.

## What to look at

| Feature | What to record |
|---|---|
| Sentence length | Shortest and longest sentence, typical range. Quote one of each. |
| Paragraph shape | Single sentences, short blocks, or long blocks. |
| Openings | How they begin a message or explanation: the request, the context, a claim, a question. |
| Stating a result | Number first, narrative first, or conclusion first. |
| Hedging | Words like "maybe", "I think", "kind of", and where they hedge or do not. |
| Directness | Imperatives, demands, or questions. Whether they challenge a claim. |
| Jargon | Platform and metric terms used without explaining them (CPA, ROAS, Smart Bidding, and the like) and which they spell out. |
| Punctuation and case | Lowercase starts, em dashes, parentheses, exclamation marks, ellipses, emoji. Record without judging typos. |
| Person | "I", "we", "you", or none. |
| Humour | Dry remarks, sarcasm, none. Quote the line. |
| Disagreement | How they push back and how they correct something. |
| Recurring phrases | Any phrase used 2 or more times. |

## Writing to the AI

Messages to an AI show word choice, directness and priorities. They do not show how the user would write for a public audience. Treat three things as usable signals only if they recur: preferred vocabulary, how bluntly they correct, and what they insist on (for example, precision, sourcing, brevity). Do not copy their chat rhythm into a post.

If the user has written standing preferences for how the AI should write to them, quote them as their stated preferences. They show what the user dislikes in prose and can seed the "never use" list, once the user confirms.

## Reporting format

For each observation:

> **[Observed]** Short sentences, mostly under 12 words. Evidence: "..." (sample 2), "..." (sample 4).
> **[Inference]** Prefers to lead with the instruction, then the reason. Evidence: "..." (sample 1). One instance; not yet a pattern.

End with the sample count, total words, confidence, and the two exercises that would most improve confidence.
