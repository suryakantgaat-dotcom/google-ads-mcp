# Voice samples

Fill this in. The skill reads it before every draft. The user had no past LinkedIn posts when this skill was built, so the sections below are empty. While they are empty, drafts are written in plain neutral prose and flagged as "voice not calibrated".

Why samples matter: credible-writer sources recommend giving the model the author's own writing instead of asking it to "sound human" (level C, not re-read). Samples help match rhythm and word choice. They do not make a post true or specific.

## How to fill this in

- Paste 3 to 10 pieces of your own writing: past posts, emails to clients, internal write-ups, Slack explanations of a test result. Informal writing is fine.
- Optionally paste 2 or 3 posts by other people whose style you want to be near. Label them as not yours. The skill will match style, not copy phrases or ideas.
