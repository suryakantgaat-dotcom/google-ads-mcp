# Measurement log

Purpose: test the defaults on your own audience. The default values in `rules-and-defaults.md` come from vendor datasets that disagree with each other (length, cadence, timing, format), so your own numbers are the only evidence that applies to you.

## How to test (level I)

- Change one variable at a time: format, length band, posting slot, hook type, CTA style, link placement.
- Keep other variables similar across the posts you compare (same post type, similar topic).
- The sources give no minimum number of posts per variant. Decide the number up front, write it in the plan, and do not stop early because one post did well. Single posts vary a lot.
- Record outcomes at a fixed point after posting (for example 7 days), not whenever you look.
- Compare against your own earlier posts, not against vendor benchmarks.

## Outcome metrics that match the goal (authority)

Impressions and reactions are weak signals of authority. Weight these more (level I):

- profile views per impression
- follower growth among target titles (hiring managers, clients, peers)
- inbound DMs and meeting requests, and what they reference
- saves
- comments from target accounts and what they say

## Review (monthly)

- Which defaults did my data support, contradict, or leave unclear?
- Which posts produced inbound conversations, and what did they have in common?
- What changed in my own practice that could explain a difference apart from the variable tested?
